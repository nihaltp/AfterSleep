# Version 1

Initial release of YT Playback Timer.

<h3>Features</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Set a playback timer for YouTube videos</li>
  <li>Automatically pauses playback when the timer ends</li>
  <li>Clean dark-themed interface</li>
  <li>Simple and lightweight design</li>
  <li>Works locally without unnecessary permissions</li>
</ul>
